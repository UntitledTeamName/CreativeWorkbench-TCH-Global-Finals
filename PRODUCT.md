# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Directors, screenwriters, novelists, and creative leads building ensemble casts, character conflicts, and narrative bibles. They operate in focused writing sprints, using the workbench as an interactive live preview companion to stories generated or shaped in WorkBuddy.

## Product Purpose

Serve as a local, distraction-free visual workbench and live preview canvas for narrative universes. It provides immediate structural visibility into character baseline psychologies, interpersonal tension edges, visual prompt kits, and scene seeds without external servers or cloud dependencies.

## Positioning

The offline-first visual workbench that transforms story concepts into structurally sound, relationship-driven narrative universes.

## Brand Personality

Warm Editorial Manuscript: tactile, professional, literary. Evokes a focused writer's desk bathed in daylight—crisp book-paper canvas, deep espresso ink, saddle leather accents, and typewriter clarity that keeps narrative relationships front and center.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS clichés such as purple gradient headings, dark-mode neon radial blooms, glowing glassmorphism, and loud metric stat cards. Gamified or cartoonish aesthetics with playful badges, bouncy physics, or toy-like cards.

## Design Principles

- Live preview first: every panel serves as an immediate, transparent window into the underlying story model without cognitive overhead.
- Interface restraint: chrome and controls recede into the background so character dynamics and story seeds take focus.
- Tactile precision: utilitarian and predictable interactions over gratuitous visual effects; every click and drag has purposeful weight.
- Offline sovereignty: completely self-contained and private, honoring writer trust with zero telemetry or network calls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Targeting WCAG AA compliance with crisp contrast ratios across all light book-paper and deep espresso text elements (>16:1 contrast for body text), full keyboard accessibility, and standard `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all layout transitions.
